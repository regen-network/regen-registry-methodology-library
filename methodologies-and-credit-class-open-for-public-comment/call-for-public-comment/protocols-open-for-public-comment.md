# Protocols Open for Public Comment

## **Currently Open for Public Comment**

**Sustainability Science has submitted "Methodology to Integrate Algae Wastewater Treatment Technologies to Avoid Emissions from Grey Infrastructure Wastewater Management Systems v1**_**"**_ **for Public Comment.  Comments can be submitted through August 26th, 2025.**

Sustainability Science is excited to partner with Gross-Wen Technologies and Regen Network to develop a carbon credit methodology for algae-integrated wastewater treatment solutions. A leader in Life Cycle Assessment for algae-based systems, Sustainability Science provides expertise in evaluating environmental impacts, while Gross-Wen Technologies brings extensive experience in system design and deployment. This protocol highlights the significant benefits of advanced algae-based nutrient removal, including greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions reductions, improved water quality, and enhanced sustainability in wastewater management.

This methodology uses the [GHG & Co-Benefits in Watershed Carbon v1.0](https://www.registry.regen.network/crediting-protocols/ghg-co-benefits-in-watershed-carbon-v1-0) protocol developed by [Virridy](https://virridy.com/).

Please reply with comments that you would like responses to in the [Public Comment Response Form](https://airtable.com/appzrw40tJdLBM2RS/shrHn8lLVSSftTQP6).

Those interested in participating in an active and real time responsive discussion can visit the [Hylo Public Comment](https://www.hylo.com/groups/regen-methodology-development/events/post/86351) page.
