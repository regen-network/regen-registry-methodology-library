# Protocols Open for Public Comment



## **Currently Open for Public Comment**

Name: _**Methodology for Soil Organic Carbon Estimation in Regenerative Cropping and Managed Grassland Ecosystems**_

Developer: [**Ecometric**](https://ecometric.co.uk)

Duration: 12/15-12/21/2025

The Regen Registry is opening public comment for an **updated version of the Ecometric methodology** titled _**Methodology for Soil Organic Carbon Estimation in Regenerative Cropping and Managed Grassland Ecosystems**_, developed by [**Ecometric**](https://ecometric.co.uk), and associated with the [**GHG Benefits in Managed Crop and Grassland Systems Credit Class**](https://www.registry.regen.network/crediting-protocols/ecometric---ghg-benefits-in-managed-crop-and-grassland-systems-credit-class).

This methodology applies a combined approach using **field-based soil sampling, remotely sensed multispectral imagery, and machine learning (Artificial Neural Networks)** to estimate changes in **Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) stocks** over time. Estimated SOC changes are converted into **CO₂-equivalent (CO₂e)** values for the purpose of greenhouse gas accounting and credit issuance.

The updated version builds upon the previously approved methodology and introduces a number of **technical clarifications and methodological enhancements** intended to improve scientific rigor, transparency, and applicability across diverse agricultural contexts. Key updates include expanded guidance on **soil stratification and sampling design**, refined treatment of **model error and uncertainty**, updated **laboratory analysis standards**, and clearer procedures for **baseline definition, net carbon change calculation, and applicable deductions** (including yield-related leakage and greenhouse gas emissions).
